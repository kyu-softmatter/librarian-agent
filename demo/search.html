What has been asked here

Zero results is an answer. searched_empty means the corpus was searched and held nothing — which is different from never having looked (not_searched), and different again from a broken tool.

The log records the fact, not a judgment. Every query is written to kb/08-retrieval/sessions/ with verdict: not_searched, because an unrecorded query is not “it was fine”. Pressing cite is what reports that a hit was actually used — that field is never inferred. There is no rating: a stored score would become a claim that one entry matters more.

That folder is gitignored on purpose. Raw query text in a public repository is a disclosure — what someone was looking for is what they are about to do — so these stay local until the publish gate is settled.
